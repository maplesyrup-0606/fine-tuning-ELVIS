General benchmarks

Accuracy in %. Δ = model − base. Seed view: values are means over the seed runs (T0.7); base also shows its sd; σ = (model mean − base mean) / base sd, i.e. how many of base's seed-to-seed standard deviations the model is away; ● = at least 2σ. Single-run view: noise band = about 2 standard errors of a difference at that sample size (cautious); ● = outside the band. MMMU = validation split; MMBench is scored per circular question group.
