General benchmarks

Accuracy in %. Δ = model − base. Seed view: values are means over the seed runs, ± = their standard deviation, and ● uses 2 standard errors of the difference of means (decoding noise only). Single-run view: Noise band = about 2 standard errors of a difference at that sample size (treats the runs as independent, so it is cautious); ● = difference larger than the band. MMMU = validation split; MMBench is scored per circular question group.
